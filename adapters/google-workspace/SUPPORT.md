# Support boundary

Google Docs reads, typed inserts, replacements, text styles, batched typed edits, and Drive exports are supported, as are Slides reads, slide create/delete/reorder, text replacement, text styles, and exports. Multi-tab Docs editing, comments and suggestions, embedded Apps Script, macros, and password or domain-restricted resources beyond what the supplied token can open are unsupported. Documents or decks whose JSON exceeds the bounded read cap, and exports larger than the 32 MB cap, are refused rather than truncated.
